Sneak Preview

Ever wonder what happened to The Amazing Chuck?!
🎩 ✨ 🪄 💥

Well, gather ‘round, ladies and gentlemen, because we’re about to pull a little MAGIC out of the past!

Our favorite down-on-his-luck magician first stumbled into the spotlight in Lacey and Lily Volume 2: Magic and Mayhem, where a seemingly ordinary magic show at Lacey’s middle school turned into one extraordinary DISASTER! With one accidental REAL magic spell, Chuck managed to switch the bodies of Lacey and her super-powered canine companion, Lily! Talk about a trick that went to the dogs!

And just when you thought Chuck couldn’t make a bigger mess of things, he made his grand exit aboard a GIANT FLYING TOP HAT!

The next time we encounter our magical misfit, he’s working at a HAUNTED PUMPKIN FARM! Wait a minute… How did he get from there to here?!? Well, THAT’S where our all-new comic book adventure comes in!

The Amazing Chuck: Special Magical Hat Trick!
🏒 😈 🎩 💨

Discover the untold misadventure that fills in the missing chapter of Chuck’s story! Somewhere between flying hats and haunted pumpkins, our bumbling magician finds himself facing off against a team of DEMONS in a winner-take-all ICE HOCKEY SHOWDOWN… with the fate of the ENTIRE UNIVERSE hanging in the balance! Magic! Monsters! Mayhem! And one spectacularly unqualified hero!
